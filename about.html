About

Alicia

Queer, neurodivergent artist based in Calgary, Alberta. Born in Canada, raised between the US and Sweden, back in Canada since 20. Drawing has been a daily constant my entire life — it's how I process, regulate, and stay well.

My work is black and white line art — high-contrast graphic designs made for stickers, prints, and digital downloads. I was diagnosed with ASD at 30, and the Honestly Autistic collection came directly out of that experience. If someone sees those designs and feels a little less alone, that's what it's for.

The Path

I started in accounting, but it never fit. I wanted flexibility, autonomy, and work that balances logic with creativity — so I moved into frontend web development.

Around the same time, I picked up digital art on my iPad. What started as experimenting became an obsession. I was producing more work than I knew what to do with, so I decided to share it. Digital downloads live on Etsy, physical products on Redbubble.
