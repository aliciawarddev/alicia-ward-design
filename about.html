About

Hi, I'm Alicia

I'm a designer and illustrator based in Calgary, Alberta. I create bold, black and white line art -- stickers, digital downloads, and prints that blend hand-drawn warmth with graphic precision.

My work spans three collections: Honestly Autistic (neurodivergent-affirming designs), Checkered (pattern-driven motifs), and Botanical (detailed plant studies). Every piece is designed to look great on stickers, apparel, and home decor.

My Process

01

Sketch

Every design starts as a rough pencil sketch. I work through multiple thumbnails to nail down the composition before committing to ink.

02

Digitize

Sketches are refined digitally, focusing on clean line work and strong contrast. I keep everything in black and white to maintain the bold graphic style.

03

Finalize

The final artwork is prepared in multiple formats -- optimized for stickers, prints, and digital downloads across Etsy and Redbubble.

Common Questions

What file formats do digital downloads include?

Each digital download includes high-resolution PNG and SVG files, suitable for personal use including printing, crafting, and digital projects.

Can I request a custom design?

I'm open to custom commissions depending on my schedule. Reach out via Etsy messages or the contact info below and we can discuss your idea.

Where can I buy physical products?

Physical products like stickers, t-shirts, mugs, and more are available through my Redbubble shop. Digital downloads are on Etsy.

Do you ship internationally?

Redbubble handles all physical product fulfillment and ships worldwide. Digital downloads on Etsy are available instantly, anywhere.

Get in Touch

For commissions, collaborations, or just to say hello -- reach out through Etsy messages or find me on social media.
