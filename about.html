About

Hi, I'm Alicia

I'm an artist and web developer based in Calgary, Alberta. I'm originally from Sweden, grew up between countries, and have lived in the US and now Canada. Being a third culture kid gave me a pretty unique way of seeing things, and that perspective finds its way into my work.

I've been drawing my entire life. Literally since I could hold a crayon. Art has always been a constant for me, not a hobby I picked up along the way, but something I've done every single day for as long as I can remember. I also paint and sculpt, though drawing is where I spend most of my time.

For me, making art isn't a nice-to-have. It's how I process things, how I regulate, how I stay well. It's a must. The digital side is newer, and sharing my work publicly is newer still, but the practice itself has been there my whole life.

How I Got Here

My first career was in accounting. I have a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Victoria, but the work never really fit the way my brain operates. I needed more flexibility and a better balance, so I made a career change into web development.

Around that same time, I started experimenting with digital art on my iPad. What began as dabbling quickly turned into an obsession. I was drawing for hours every day, producing more work than I knew what to do with. At some point I realized I might as well share it.

This isn't a side hustle. I just make a lot of art and wanted to put it somewhere.

The Honestly Autistic Story

I spent most of my life thinking I was neurotypical and not understanding why certain things felt so hard. When I was 30, I first suspected I might be neurodivergent. That same year I was diagnosed autistic. It was a huge identity shift, but so much finally made sense.

The Honestly Autistic collection came directly out of that experience. These designs are about the overstimulation, the masking, the need for space, all the stuff that's hard to explain but easy to recognize if you live it. I wanted to be honest and genuine about it. If even one person sees these and feels a little less alone, that's enough.

My Process

01

Sketch

Every design starts as a rough digital sketch on the iPad. I work through multiple thumbnails to nail down the composition before committing to clean lines.

02

Refine

The sketch gets built up into final line art, focusing on clean strokes and strong contrast. Everything stays black and white to keep the bold graphic style consistent.

03

Publish

The final artwork is exported in multiple formats, optimized for stickers, prints, and digital downloads across Etsy and Redbubble.
