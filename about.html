About the Artist

Hi, I'm Alicia Ward—an artist based in Calgary, Alberta creating geometric and botanical paintings with crisp line work, intricate details, and bold contrast.

Work With Me

My Story

Art has been part of my life since I could hold a pen. As a neurodivergent person, creating isn't just a passion—it's a necessary outlet for expression and mental well-being.

Originally from Sweden, I moved to Canada in 2014 and settled in Calgary, drawn by the mountains, dry climate, and clear skies. My background spans accounting and web development, but art has always been the constant thread.

My Work

Minimalist and geometric, sometimes colorful, sometimes monochromatic. Each piece is entirely handmade and one of a kind—in an increasingly digital world, I'm committed to work that requires time, care, and human touch.

I work on stretched canvas (8×10" to 16×20"), varnished for protection. I'm drawn to geometric patterns for their precision and balance, and botanical subjects for their meditative, organic forms.

My Process

Every painting represents hours of focused effort to achieve the precision and quality I envision. This detail-oriented approach means each piece isn't just art—it's a reflection of dedication to craft.

Let's Create Something Together

I'm available for commissions and custom work—whether you're looking for a specific geometric pattern, botanical piece, or something uniquely tailored to your space.
