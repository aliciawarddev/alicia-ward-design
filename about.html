About

The Artist

Hi, I'm Alicia Ward, an artist based in Calgary, Alberta. I create geometric and botanical paintings with crisp line work, intricate details, and bold contrast using acrylics and pens on canvas.

My Story

Art has been part of my life since I could hold a pen as a toddler. I've taken art classes throughout my education—from grade school through university, always using electives to continue my practice. As a neurodivergent person, creating isn't just a passion—it's a necessary outlet for expression and mental well-being. My work reflects this deep connection: clean, minimalist compositions that balance creative freedom with meticulous attention to detail.

I'm a queer, neurodivergent, multicultural artist. Originally from Sweden, I moved to Canada in 2014 and eventually settled in Calgary, drawn by the proximity to the mountains, the dry climate, and the clear skies after years of grey weather in Stockholm and Victoria. My background includes an accounting career followed by a transition to web development, but art has always been the constant thread throughout my life.

My Work

My aesthetic is minimalist and geometric, sometimes colorful, sometimes monochromatic. I'm inspired by nature, the work of other artists, and the unexpected moments of beauty in everyday life. Each piece I create is entirely handmade and one of a kind—in an increasingly digital world, I'm committed to offering work that requires time, care, and human touch.

I work primarily on stretched canvas in sizes ranging from 8x10 to 16x20 inches, and all pieces are varnished for protection and longevity. I'm drawn to geometric patterns because of their visual satisfaction—the precision, the balance, the way they come together. Botanical subjects connect deeply with me as an avid plant lover, and there's something meditative about capturing the organic forms and details of nature. I launched this body of work in 2025.

My Process

I hold incredibly high standards for my work. Every painting represents hours of focused effort to achieve the precision and quality I envision. This detail-oriented approach means each piece isn't just art—it's a reflection of dedication to craft.

Work With Me

I'm available for commissions and custom work. Whether you're looking for a specific geometric pattern, botanical piece, or something uniquely tailored to your space, I'd love to collaborate with you.
