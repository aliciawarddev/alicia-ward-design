About

Hi, I'm Alicia

I'm a queer, neurodivergent artist based in Calgary, Alberta. I've been drawing my entire life — a daily constant, not something I came to later. I paint and sculpt too, though drawing is where I spend most of my time.

My work is black and white line art — high-contrast graphic designs made for stickers, prints, and digital downloads. The digital side is newer, and sharing publicly is newer still, but the practice itself has been there my whole life.

Making art is how I process things, how I regulate, how I stay well.

I was born in Canada, grew up mostly between the US and Sweden, and have been back in Canada since I was 20. Growing up between cultures gives you a particular way of seeing things — one that finds its way into my work.

How I Got Here

I started out in accounting, but it never really fit the way my brain works or what I actually enjoy. I wanted flexibility, autonomy, and work that balances logic with creativity, so I transitioned into frontend web development.

Around that same time, I started experimenting with digital art on my iPad. What began as dabbling quickly turned into an obsession. I was drawing for hours every day, producing more work than I knew what to do with, so I decided to share it.

The shops are a newer part of the practice. Right now they're a way to share the work and over time the goal is for them to sustain themselves.

The Honestly Autistic Story

I spent most of my life not understanding why certain things felt so hard. At 30, I was diagnosed with ASD, and a lot suddenly made sense.

The Honestly Autistic collection came directly out of that experience. The designs are about overstimulation, masking, the need for space, the stuff that's hard to explain but easy to recognize if you live it. If someone sees these and feels a little less alone, that's what it's for.

The Collections

Honestly Autistic

Neurodivergent-affirming designs drawn from personal experience. Funny, honest, and meant for anyone who gets it.

Checkered

Pattern-driven designs with bold black and white checkerboard motifs. Moths, flowers, paint splats, smiley faces, familiar subjects given a graphic treatment.

Botanical

Detailed line art studies of tropical leaves, monstera, pothos, banana leaf. Clean forms with an emphasis on natural patterns and textures.

Spaces & Places

Rooms, windows, shelves, spaces that feel lived in. This collection focuses on interior and exterior environments through detailed line art.

When I'm Not Drawing

I like being outside. Walking, running, hiking, camping. I enjoy a quiet lifestyle. When I'm indoors, I'm probably rewatching a comfort show for the hundredth time.
