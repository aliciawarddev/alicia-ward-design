Policies

Everything you need to know about ordering, shipping, and caring for your artwork.

Shipping

All paintings ship from Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

Returns & Exchanges

Due to the handmade, one-of-a-kind nature of my work, all sales are final. I do not accept returns or exchanges.

If your painting arrives damaged, please contact me within 48 hours with photos of the damage and packaging. I'll work with you to find a solution.

Commissions

Custom commissions require a 50% deposit to begin work, with the remaining balance due upon completion before shipping.

Turnaround time varies based on size and complexity—typically 2–4 weeks. I'll provide an estimated timeline when we discuss your project.

Care Instructions

All paintings are varnished for protection, but should be kept out of direct sunlight to prevent fading over time.

Dust gently with a soft, dry cloth. Avoid moisture and harsh cleaning products.

Payment

I accept payment via PayPal and major credit cards. Full payment is required before shipping for gallery purchases.

Artwork Condition

Each painting is handmade and may contain minor imperfections, variations, or unique characteristics that are part of the artistic process. By purchasing, you accept the artwork in its current condition as shown in the listing photos.
