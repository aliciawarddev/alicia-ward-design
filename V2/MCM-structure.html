About Me

Artist & Designer

I create artwork that explores the intersection of geometric precision and organic forms. Working primarily in digital media on iPad and with traditional acrylics on canvas, my style focuses on clean lines, tessellations, and botanical studies.

Each piece reflects a minimalist aesthetic that celebrates pattern, color, and the beauty of structured design. My work ranges from intricate geometric patterns to delicate botanical illustrations, all unified by a commitment to clarity and intentional composition.

"Art is the elimination of the unnecessary."
